Sliabh Liath is a Mountain Range in the Western Peninsula of Tírdraiocht
It Runs North to South for approximately 20kilometers. Cutting the Shores of the Sunset Coast from the Long Lough, except by travelling around the Southern and Northern Passage.

The Northern Passage is guarded by Fort Greymoor. While the last settlement on the Southern range is Baile. The Settlement of [[Caer Ciarán]] lies in the foothills of the Eastern side of the mountain.

Deep within is rumoured to be the [[Echoing Hollow|Echoing Hollows]], a mine of legend where [[Riarann]] the Smith pulled the metal [[Iarnchroi]] from the earth and forged the first tools, and eventually the first weapons of war in the young days of the land

The Grey River flows rapidly from the mountain south east towards the Port City of Deepwater and empties in to the Long Lough.
